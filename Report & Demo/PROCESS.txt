REPORT AND DEMO PROCESS

Purpose:
Prepare the final written submission and optional demo evidence.

What to do in this folder:
1. Write a 7 to 9 page report in DOCX and PDF formats.
2. Include dataset details, source links, methods, cleaning steps, charts, database work, findings, and conclusions.
3. Add screenshots of database queries, charts, and important analysis outputs.
4. Explain the project story in a way a general reader can follow.
5. Include clear recommendations or actionable conclusions.
6. Prepare optional demo notes or a short video showing the repository, code, database, and final charts.

- NDTA631_Group_Report.pdf
- demo_script.txt
- screenshots/
