DATASET NOTES

Dataset:
Prevalence of severe food insecurity in the population (%)

File:
WB_WDI_SN_ITK_SVFI_ZS.csv

Source:
World Bank Data360 / World Development Indicators

How it should be used:
1. Filter for South Africa records where REF_AREA is ZAF or REF_AREA_LABEL is South Africa.
2. Use TIME_PERIOD as the year.
3. Use OBS_VALUE as the percentage prevalence value.
4. Confirm that UNIT_MEASURE_LABEL is Percentage before reporting the result.
5. Sort the records by year before calculating trends.

Suggested analysis:
- Track severe food insecurity prevalence over time.
- Calculate year-to-year change where multiple years are available.
- Compare the trend with the IPC food insecurity classification findings.
