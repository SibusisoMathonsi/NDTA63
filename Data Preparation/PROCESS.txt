DATA PREPARATION PROCESS

Purpose:
Prepare the raw food-security datasets for analysis while keeping the original CSV files unchanged.

What to do in this folder:
1. Load the raw datasets from the Raw datasets folder.
2. Filter the data for South Africa records where the analysis requires country-specific results.
3. Rename unclear columns into readable names.
4. Check for missing values, duplicate records, inconsistent dates, and incorrect data types.
5. Convert date and time fields into usable formats.
6. Create cleaned CSV files for later analysis.
7. Produce descriptive statistics such as count, mean, minimum, maximum, and standard deviation.
8. Write short notes explaining every important cleaning decision.

Suggested outputs:
- cleaned_food_security_phase.csv
- cleaned_severe_food_insecurity.csv
- data_quality_notes.txt
- descriptive_statistics.csv

Marking link:
This folder supports the 15 marks for Data Preparation.
